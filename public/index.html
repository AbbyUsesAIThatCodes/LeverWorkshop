YOUR WORKBENCH

Try a little change.

  1. Drag a load or the pivot to another hole.
  2. Let go. Watch which side drops.
  3. Use a side slider to add or remove gears.
  4. Try the same change on your real lever.

The beam is held level while you drag a part. Each load stays on its own side of the pivot. The pivot uses two neighboring hole columns.

Look around: drag the table to orbit all the way around. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Use Fit view if you get lost.

Keyboard: choose Load A, Pivot, or Load B below, then use the position slider. The arrow keys move one hole at a time. Use Tab to reach the gear sliders.

A and B stay with their parts as the camera turns. Point at a gear slider to light up its load.

TEACHER SETTINGS

About this model

Gravity acts on the beam, connectors, brackets, gears, and pins. Moving the pivot changes the beam’s own turning effect too.

Masses are approximate. Bracket and gear-pack masses come from the build packet. Beam, pin, and connector masses are estimated from the CAD. We have not calibrated shaft friction against your classroom lever.

The high-mounted loads can tip farther after a small tilt. Each change starts level. Use Hold level to set up an arrangement, then release it.

Adjust measured masses and motion

Gear-pack masses include three pins. Bracket mass includes its five permanent pins. All masses are grams.

Your arrangement and settings stay in this browser. No names, accounts, or results are collected.
