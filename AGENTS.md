# Lever Workshop working agreement

- Keep the app a small, static browser workshop suitable for GitHub Pages and school Chromebooks.
- The current release is one full-window exploration interface. Directly drag both loads and the pivot; use discrete real mounting positions. No lesson gates, scores, timers, or Challenge mode in this PR.
- A load cannot cross the pivot; the pivot cannot cross either load. Its two mounting columns stay clear of load brackets.
- Share assembly transforms between rendering and mechanics. Verify connector holes against CAD geometry. Do not mistake visual resemblance for physical verification.
- Model the moving beam, connectors, brackets, gears, and pins. Keep measured inputs, packet approximations, and inferred defaults distinct. Document calibration assumptions and remaining physical checks.
- No student-facing Newton calculations. Gear packs are real recipes, not equal weight units; 17 g and 22 g are not a 1:2 pair.
- Full horizontal camera orbit. Sliders and labels track physical A/B identity from every camera angle.
- Keep every action on its own line or control. Prefer plain language, Comic Sans first, bundled Comic Neue fallback, and question numbers followed by `)` when needed.
- Bold key terms; combine bold/underline/italic sparingly for critical directions.
- Never put private curriculum PDFs, rosters, or student identities in this public repository.
- Validate meaningful mechanics and browser behavior. Report browser checks, physical checks, and instructional readiness separately.
- Challenge mode is the following PR; preserve the requirements in docs/ROADMAP.md. Every future puzzle must have a solution reachable with its permitted controls.
- Do not merge or deploy this development PR. The reviewable deliverable is a PR with verification and screenshots.
